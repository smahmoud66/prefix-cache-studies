# Exact continuation evaluator

[Read the paper](../papers/evaluator/paper.pdf) · [LaTeX source](../papers/evaluator/main.tex)

One request charges its missing prefix blocks at entry, admits the whole path atomically and evicts by LRU. The evaluator scores known, finite continuations whose next action can depend on cache hits. Candidates tied for best immediate work can have different complete exit states. Only the historical scalar score is shared; the winning candidate commits its own complete exit.

In the 128-block tool/agent decision condition the two shortcuts eliminated 82.6% of replays relative to full replay. The complete-state and focused-state implementations share both shortcuts and agree on replay counts by construction. In that condition the focused-state implementation used 25.2% less complete native CPU, and local caller wall time fell 16.9%. Mean native CPU favored focused-state propagation in six of eight conditions. The author implemented both variants and used three repetitions on one Apple M2 after scheduling outcomes were known. The Mooncake historical policy saved 0.204% at 128 blocks and 1.001% at 32. Both external Qwen episodes tied immediate ordering. [Reproduction guide](reproduce.md)
