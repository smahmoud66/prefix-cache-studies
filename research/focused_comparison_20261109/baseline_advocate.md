# Prospective baseline audit

The focused-LRU adaptation for atomic-prefix evaluation uses the concentrated younger-set semantics of Touzeau et al., *Fast and Exact Analysis for LRU Caches*, §§4.1–4.3, as inspected in the earlier manuscript. The paper studies scalar cache access and program control flow. Our adaptation evaluates a finite scheduling continuation from concrete candidate exits. [Archived passage comparison](../exact_state_manuscript_20261109/manuscript.md#relation-to-existing-exact-abstractions)

**Derivation.** An old resident tracked identity x has a set Y(x) of younger identities. All path nodes are charged against the old state. Protected admission of a path P places every path member ahead of old survivors outside P while preserving old outsider order. A surviving x outside P therefore has exactly Y(x) union P ahead, and it survives exactly when that union has fewer than C members. An absent x outside P stays absent. The ith admitted path member has exactly the first i−1 path identities ahead. These rules update one correlated family of observations and determine the next k-LPM choice from its own simulated cache. Joining observations independently from different candidates would lose that property.

**Strong adaptation.** Only members of the known pair support U will be touched. The tracked IDs earlier than x together with the number of younger outsider IDs fully determine its younger-set update after outsider erasure. Listing tracked IDs with their absolute ranks encodes both pieces. This justifies ordinary exact equality-class memoization, also including common score terms whenever the interface requests an absolute value. For an untouched tracked resident, its count of younger outsider IDs can never increase. If the initial maximum such count is z, and u is the true number of labels served, then at most u−1+z younger identities can precede any tracked resident before its first touch. After first touch at most u−1 can. Thus u+z≤C implies u−h misses for h initially resident tracked labels, including h=0. This certificate follows directly from the focused observations and deserves inclusion in FOCUSED. No broader paper-level claim about the authors' implementation or the first use of the certificate follows.

**Operational fairness.** The timed native FOCUSED receives the same candidate enumeration, class discovery, common-term elision and certificate as RANK-11. It must convert only actual residual witnesses, never every candidate eagerly. The fallback builds a compact correlated list of tracked identities with their absolute ranks in one pass, then advances it without storing or iterating outsider entries. Two fixed capacity-bounded buffers alternate without list copying. The compiled kernel reuses the same transition mark vector and epoch counter as full-state replay. It charges candidate conversion, membership probes, on-demand historical set construction, sparse updates and all memory. Conditional hit-length decisions, tie rules and committed real cache states have the original meanings. The old RANK-11 implementation takes the other straightforward option of retaining one concrete full witness for each residual key. Its replay multiplicity is already accessible to the strong focused adaptation. Runtime differences can still reflect sparse propagation versus concrete witness replay. RANK-01 is an informative weaker comparator for the additional work eliminated by the certificate. No known-support shortcut requiring future observations or hit/miss-only subsumption is introduced.

**Implementation caveat.** Fixed local arrays give predictable storage for the two sparse buffers, with stack peak proportional to the compiled physical capacity even at lower occupied size. Their live tracked counts can be much smaller. Initial conversion and rank arithmetic can cost a sparse kernel more than full-state replay when a large fraction of the cache is relevant. The selection history and memo store are identical for the two strong implementations. Their comparison cannot establish an intrinsic lower bound or an advantage of the certificate over focused principles.
