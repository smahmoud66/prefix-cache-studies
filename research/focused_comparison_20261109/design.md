## Focused semantics and their consequences

Fix capacity C, an entry state and an ordered path P of distinct canonical prefix identities capped at C. All hits are charged at entry. For each x in the known continuation support U, let Y(x) contain all identities ahead of x if x is resident, and let it be absent otherwise. Keep this family jointly for one execution. Each path identity pᵢ ends with exactly its i−1 preceding path labels ahead of it. For a previously resident x outside P its new preceding set, *before truncation*, is P together with its old preceding labels outside P, exactly Y(x) ∪ P. It survives iff that set has size < C. Absent x outside P stays absent. Applying all these rules to the old observations simultaneously yields the entry-charged atomic step. The resulting joint observations determine the next k-LPM choice and its tie priority. Induction therefore gives the exact adaptive order and miss total. Independently merging per-label sets or applying general hit/miss subsumption could create an impossible observation vector or lose an integer cost.

Because P ⊆ U, no identity outside U will ever be touched. For each resident x retain the tracked members of Y(x) and only the **number** of outsiders it contains. Equivalently store resident tracked identities in their joint absolute-rank order with rank gaps: earlier tracked IDs recover the tracked younger members, and rank minus one minus their count recovers the outsider count. This is a lossless recoding of the outsider-erased focused observations for this objective. For an untouched tracked resident at old rank r, its prospective new rank is |P| + r − count of touched old ranks ≤ r. Truncate at C. This supplies an efficient sparse propagation rule, including when ranks have gaps. There is no need for a quadratic table of younger sets in the production comparator. The independent literal focused-state oracle retains the full identities to test this derivation.

Let u be the exact number of distinct labels in the paths actually served, h the number initially resident, and z the largest number of outside-U identities initially ahead of an initially resident tracked label (zero when h=0). Before its first touch, such a label has at most z younger outsiders and at most u−1 other tracked labels ahead. Once first touched it has only tracked labels ahead. Thus u+z≤C guarantees that all initially resident tracked labels survive to first use, and all subsequent uses hit; missing blocks total u−h. This is an elementary sufficient survival bound in focused younger-set terms. Matching per-pair known-support keys can also share a score by the same focused transition induction. A common term may be omitted for decisions; absolute totals must restore it. Each action commits its own complete exit.

Use the frozen current-cohort incumbent, shared exact candidate enumeration and full-state deduplication with one preferred representative per eligible full exit. Preserve exact integer scoring and correspondence to all candidate actions. Both rank toggles follow the existing rank implementation: on a residual class miss its continuation is evaluated from the class's full witness with the frozen transition. When certification is off, skip all direct tests. When sharing is off, skip varying-rank construction, common-term skipping and cross-exit memoization. The primary measured comparison deliberately distinguishes two useful ways to execute a residual replay. Any timing difference between them is implementation-dependent; no intrinsic separation in their replay counts or achievable score reuse is predicted.

## Work, storage and three reachable cases

Let k be the current cohort size (≤4), E the number of its distinct immediately optimal exits, C retained slots, u the size of a historical pair's union, g its number of requests, B its total capped-path occurrences and H the stored occurrences across the history window. Let D be the number of distinct needed projection classes among E exits and S the number of exits directly certifiable. D′ counts required distinct uncertified classes. Let I count distinct identities seen in the episode and v the number of ranks differing among exits. All bounds below are for one pair after enumeration. Every method pays for the same incumbent calculation and up to Σ k!/(k−d)! partial-order nodes over depths d=0…k; deduplication in the linear full-state table can cost O(k! E C). Shared transition cache space is O(VC) for V partial states actually allocated, plus interning and timestamp scratch.

FULL/RANK-00 incurs up to E full replays. Denote a replay cost by R, bounded by O(B + g[C+k log(C+1)]) for the fixed array family. RANK-10 adds up to O(EC) for certification and replays E−S times. RANK-01 computes rank differences once per decision in O(EC), builds per-exit keys over at most v positions, and replays D times. Its linear-vector lookup can cost O(E D (v+1)); initial key scans can cost O(Ev). RANK-11 pays the certificate scans as well, builds keys only for uncertified exits and replays D′ times, with lookup work O((E−S)D′(v+1)). These are upper bounds; size rejection u>C is constant-time, common terms can disappear entirely from decision-only scoring, and a sole eligible action needs no historical score in decision mode. A common term needed absolutely requires at most one replay or one direct evaluation in sharing modes. The number of direct successes actually attempted can thus differ from the hypothetical S across all exits. Direct hits and common skips can overlap. There is no general additive savings law.

Both strong methods pay the per-decision rank-difference work and pair-local memo space O(D′(v+1)), plus O(E+C) vector/scratch storage. The history membership index and pair-union counts process arriving/departing path occurrences, amortized over history updates. Looking up support membership in the compiled bit-mask index is constant time for the fixed 16-cohort history. Building a union cold would inspect B occurrences. The actual index grows with I, so space is O(H+I) in addition to the engine's shared interning/scratch. FULL can retain historical paths without the added membership index, O(H). RANK-10 needs membership and certificate scans but no projected-key memo. The focused replay converts each full witness that reaches the fallback by scanning up to C slots. A sparse joint rank list visits the resident tracked entries rather than all named outsiders on each subsequent transition. Its update work is bounded by the present tracked list plus path size, plus adaptive hit probing; actual cost depends on the kernel and how many tracked labels remain. A vector of literal size-C younger sets for each of u identities would pay a different, avoidable cost.

- **Sharing alone can help.** From cold, serving singleton roots x,y,a at C=3 in two orders can give (a,x,y) and (a,y,x) with equal immediate cost. A historical pair accessing b,a,c,d then a has u=4>C. Both keys contain only a at rank one; one replay suffices for these two exits, and the pair can be omitted if common across the whole candidate set.
- **Certification can help across different keys.** At C=3, start cold and serve a,b; the two optimal orders leave (a,b) and (b,a). Replay singleton a then c. The keys give different ranks for a. Both satisfy u+z≤3, and each loses only c once. A projection memo by itself cannot merge these two keys.
- **Neither helps.** At C=2 the same current orders give (a,b) and (b,a). Replay (c,a) then (d), with the registered k-LPM selector. The two keys differ on a, u=3>C, and the respective pair charges are two and three. All classes need replay and projection work is additional. Constructions establish possibility, not workload frequency.
