## Contribution map and falsification

The deployable question is whether directly comparing feasible orderings of a small, already arrived cohort using the retained prefix state reduces full-trace prefill positions versus the strongest queue-aware ordering at matched retained capacity and displacement. Start a bounded search from FCFS, k-LPM, cLPM and/or the cohort-adapted shadow DFS incumbents. Score a whole feasible ordering by actual contiguous-prefix misses under the common cache transition. One admissible lower bound at a partial branch is cost so far plus the number of distinct not-yet-resident blocks required by the remaining requests (scaled by 512 plus unseen tails). Prune when it cannot better the incumbent, use a fixed expansion cap, and return the least cost completed order found. This ensures conditional cohort cost no larger than any included incumbent *from that same entry state*. The eventual cache contents differ. Full-stream costs can therefore worsen. Charge sorting, hash inspection, provenance processing, search nodes, CPU seconds, and retained/current-request memory separately. The zero-expansion incumbent selector is the mechanism ablation and a particularly strong cheap primary comparator.

A hand-derived warm-cache witness uses C=3, cached {a, a/a2, b}, ready requests a/a2/a3 and b (append compulsory tails). Longest-hit-first computes two blocks; b first computes one. A shadow DFS can already recover this gain. At cold start with enough capacity to retain a prompt, DFS can attain the distinct-block floor. Natural time clustering, no sharing, large retained caches, insufficient backlog or altered next-cohort state can defeat additional planning. Strictly positive effects in both trace populations versus a tuned/strong incumbent and practical planner CPU cost are empirical issues; a sole favorable small-cache cell is insufficient. These traces establish a finite population; contiguous segment heterogeneity does not make thousands of requests or two traces independent organizations or production days.

Secondary diagnostic from cold start: let D sum distinct demanded full blocks over chronologically ordered cohorts. For each block present in consecutive demand cohorts i<j, introduce one interval of boundary cuts [i,j). Let H be the maximum number selectable with at most C on any cut. Each cohort's first demand either computes the block or inherited continuous residency across all intervening cuts. Gaps for each block cannot charge a single recomputation twice. Thus 512(D−H)+sum of tails is a lower bound on total prefill for *any* permutation inside the cohorts and any eviction policy, under the declared source of blocks. The relaxed oracle allows free sharing inside each cohort and arbitrary block persistence satisfying cut capacity. A standard capacitated interval flow solves H, or sweep intervals by start and discard the latest-ending active interval at overcapacity. Initial warm blocks require dummy entry intervals or a conservative subtraction; omitting the entry correction is invalid. This certificate can be loose because it ignores in-cohort capacity, contiguous-prefix constraints and evictions during service. It bounds possible further improvement relative to any feasible end-to-end schedule measured under matching assumptions. The novelty claim concerns the combined experiment and the order-uncertainty relaxation only.
