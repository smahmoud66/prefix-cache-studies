## Token scheduling

**Derived bound.** Fix ordered dispatch cohorts. For each path-qualified, causally valid full block identity b, select one actual demand in each cohort in which it is guaranteed to occur. D is the number of selected demands across all b. Select a gap between successive representatives of b if there is no creation of b in the half-open gap (after the earlier representative through the later one). This entails residency on every boundary cut between those cohorts. At any cut no more than C distinct selected intervals can cross it. Intervals for the same b are disjoint on cuts. With an initial cache I, an initial resident b also has a candidate interval from the initial cut through the cut before its first demand. A first cold demand must incur at least one creation. Every other unselected gap either incurs at least one new creation, or, for a first initially warm demand, can be covered by the initial interval if no such creation occurs. Gaps partition each block's attributable creations, so no creation counted this way can pay twice. Thus every admissible schedule has at least D−H creations, where H is the maximum number of packable intervals subject to the cut capacities and initial eligibility. Giving each warm first demand a free hit instead is a weaker valid correction. For an initially warm demand in cohort 1 its interval uses the initial cut. A simple sorted-start unit-weight interval sweep can discard a largest-ending active interval on overflow, or an interval network LP can be used. The substitute interval ends no later and cannot increase occupancy of future cuts. The certificate relaxes within-cohort order and prefix closure and need not be attained.

The proof assumes identifiable prefix-qualified KV blocks, no free external restore, no persistent scratch outside C and accountable creations. Scratch serving the current request is compatible. A window of rank displacement alone does not guarantee a request occurs in its nominal chronological segment. Preassembled, nonoverlapping eligible cohorts are sufficient. With time-limited admission, count a final partial cohort and explicitly account for the gate and external backlog. Bound dispatch displacement in positions. A displacement bound cannot imply a bound in seconds of queue service delay. Charged partial tail tokens belong outside the full-block bound if never reusable. For prompts longer than C, pinning the full path in committed capacity is impossible. Specify the admit/release rules, partial-block identity and oversized paths before freezing.

**Hand comparisons.** Scratch is local to one request and committed cache is prefix-closed. These are designed examples, not study outcomes.

- Strict advantage over greedy LPM even with all cohort prompts visible. Let C=3, I={a,b,b/y}, and demand paths b/y/z and a. The former has two matching blocks, so LPM serves it, pins its entire path, and evicts a. Cost 1+1. Serving a first costs 0, then extending b/y costs 1. This demonstrates a physical mechanism without predicting the result against a multi-template incumbent.
- Cross-cohort counterexample to global dominance of exact cohort minimization. Let C=2, initial path a/z, first cohort [b/t,a/x], second [a/x]. Serving a/x then b/t costs 1+2 locally, versus 2+2 in the opposite order. The second cohort costs 2 versus 0, so the lower-cost local choice loses on the trace, 5 versus 4.
- Counterexample to certificate exactness with fully flexible eviction including bypass. Let C=1, cold, and use [A], [B,B], [A]. Here D−H=3−1=2. Keeping A across the middle cohort requires computing B twice. Retaining B requires computing the final A again. Optimum 3.
- Under a different per-request bound of one overtaking event, C=2 warm b/s, queue [a/t,b/x,b/x] has greedy LPM cost 1+2+2=5 versus FIFO 2+2+0=4. This example does not transfer unchanged to a gated three-request cohort, since the two b/x requests can then both run first.
- Cold isolated cohort, C at least the maximum request depth, and current path retained at completion: prefix-trie depth-first order achieves exactly one creation per distinct node. Each subtree is contiguous, so each edge is entered once. This supplies a strong cheap incumbent and an exact sanity case.

**Classification and smallest corrections.** Fatal to a proposed general novelty claim: known bounded-delay reorderable paging, known offline flow representations, and PEEK's co-designed queue/eviction already cover those principles. A comparison of exact short-horizon policy search at identical cache/queue information and a certified headroom analysis on gated prefix requests remains testable. Bounded corrections: hold eviction and allowed scheduling constraints constant across planners, establish timestamp cohort closure independently of observed savings, account for controller CPU, model block length/tails/over-C explicitly, and rank the baseline templates before outcomes. Exact w! permutations is exact for this single cohort and this fixed eviction rule. Headroom is baseline cost minus a lower bound on optimal cost. A large headroom is an upper bound on possible improvement. It does not establish attainable savings. Uncertainties to measure include occupancy, scarcity of simultaneously eligible shared prefixes, global carryover, certificate tightness and controller work.

**Statistical units.** Predesign fixed disjoint temporal episodes, a common cold start or specified common prehistory, and a grouping-preserving development split. A census yields a finite-panel estimand. For a predeclared simple random sample without replacement from a finite frame of episodes, the design-based mean paired difference and sampling-variance estimate with finite-population correction are valid even when episodes have cross-time correlation. A normal/t approximation is an interval approximation whose limitations matter for skewed sparse gains. Adjacent requests/blocks do not provide independent replicates. A ratio of sums requires ratio-estimator inference or a known common denominator. A mean of episode-normalized differences is a different estimand; specify which.

## Visualization/clarification

**Derived result.** With a public prior on hidden executable predicates h and a squared count estimation loss, write N=N(h) and p=Pr(test outcome yes). The reduction in posterior Bayes risk under an exact membership query equals p(1−p)(E[N|yes]−E[N|no])² by total variance. With a single remaining query this calculation is exact classical decision-theoretic value of information. A fixed-horizon dynamic program using the same public information is a deployable comparator when computationally feasible. A hidden-filter exact count uses extra information.

For revised robust formulation, assume unknown binary membership is within r Hamming flips of some predicate h over the finite table. Given s audited positives, m_h audited disagreements, u unaudited rows, and a_h predicted positives among unaudited rows, h with m_h>r is impossible. For each feasible h the exact attainable totals are all integers from s+max(0,a_h−r+m_h) to s+min(u,a_h+r−m_h). One can attain each extreme by directing unspent flips to positives or negatives. The union over h is the exact identified set, potentially disconnected. The endpoints define an exact hull for minimax absolute count loss. If a reported integer is mandatory, the minimax loss radius is ceil((max−min)/2). An empty set is a model rejection; a nonempty set does not establish that r was valid. For a singleton h=0 with r=1, any series of negative answers can leave [0,1] intact until the last possible exceptional unseen row is removed.

**Classification and smallest corrections.** Fatal to claims about recovering truth/intent from the projection alone: high-dimensional rows with identical 2D values can receive opposite membership labels. Fatal to a new general risk-directed query principle: the objective is classical and the visual worker found direct active surveying work. Bounded correction: use the conditional Hamming-ball target, a registered scripted membership oracle, public predicates and matched generic LLM, exact known-filter oracle, exact minimax/DP where affordable, and deterministic same-input baselines. Deduplicate semantically identical predicates or declare their prior mass. Score parser representation and count separately. Audit user-supplied answers separately from tool calls. Hidden-rule realizability and validity of r are assumptions, and controlled heuristic deviations cannot establish how humans describe clinical intent. The incremental value of language and the projected brush, and shrinkage under plausible disagreement, are uncertainties. The robust target has a potentially interpretable limited ambiguity guarantee, but an unmotivated r or table-to-person transfer remains weaker than the directly measurable request workload.
