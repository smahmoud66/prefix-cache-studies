# Contribution assessment

**Strongest supported claim.** Under protected atomic prefix admission and ordinary leaf LRU, resident prefix identities with their absolute recency ranks suffice to evaluate a finite adaptive continuation whose cache observations factor through that projection. The same representation provides a sufficient certificate for computing the exact miss count directly when every path in the specified workload is served. Sharing scores between candidate exits preserves the original dispatch rule. The matched native implementation reproduced 8,766 cohort–capacity comparisons and reduced mean CPU per cohort from 283.03 to 68.72 µs at 128 blocks on exposed Mooncake traffic. [Correctness argument and synthesis](manuscript.md) · [Matched benchmark](../exact_tie_20261108/final_report.md)

**Classification: a useful specialization with a measured implementation payoff.** Scalar LRU stack analysis, exact focused-cache observations and composition with observation-compatible policies have precedents. The substantive distinction in the inspected passages is the combination of entry-time charging for an entire prefix path, a joint survival certificate and exact multi-exit scoring with preservation of deterministic dispatch ties. The proofs explain a concrete reduction in the number of historical replays. The experiments support a runtime advantage against full-state replay on one recorded panel. Priority for a broader abstraction or a globally minimal representation is unestablished. [Closest inspected results](manuscript.md#relation-to-existing-exact-abstractions)

**Scope of the empirical claim.** Earlier tool/agent traffic benefited from the history-informed policy. On external chat, thousands of cohorts offered different immediately optimal exits, yet the frozen scorer kept the immediate incumbent at primary capacity. External API traffic yielded a structural tie under lineage isolation. Both external episodes added decision time over immediate ordering, with zero incremental prefill savings. These results leave the evaluator’s exactness intact and provide no deployment claim. [External result audit](../external_tie_20261109/result_audit.md)

**Single most important unresolved issue for a future submission.** How much additional evaluation work does the joint certificate and per-continuation state sharing eliminate **beyond a direct adaptation of established focused-LRU analysis to the identical atomic-prefix objective**? The existing comparator replays each complete exit. It measures the value of the combined implementation against that reference, while leaving the incremental scientific advantage over the closest abstraction unresolved.
