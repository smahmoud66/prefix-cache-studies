# Independent equivalence and timing audit

The checks use artificial tries. They read only frozen source, native binary and integrity records from the parent study. All synthetic paths contain unique node IDs along each ancestor chain. The test assigns a service timestamp to every node in the current path, counts the initial cached prefix and repeatedly evicts the oldest unprotected leaf, breaking equal timestamps by depth and canonical ID. Distinct old timestamps reconstructed from a reachable MRU tuple preserve eviction priority. It enumerates current permutations without pruning or candidate-state grouping. The frozen Python portfolio and exact search supply the deterministic immediate incumbent. Historical k-LPM is implemented separately by scanning pending paths for consecutive resident prefixes with first-arrival tie priority. A historical pair starts from its candidate exit and each cohort restarts its alternating service cycle. Independent exact minimization first prefers the incumbent, then the earliest lexicographic order. [Test source](test_exact.py)

The frozen binary SHA256 is checked against its original freeze. Its per-input selected action, incumbent, complete exit, immediate blocks, denominator and advantage in integer blocks are compared against the oracle. The frozen binary omits an action-independent score constant on single-exit calls. The oracle restores all constants for absolute comparisons against the new native reference and optimized implementations. Both historical modes are exercised with and without absolute scoring. k-LPM and exact-immediate are checked for action, full exit and work. ## Synthetic coverage

- Exhaustive cross-products cover all 3 capacity-one and all 6 capacity-two reachable MRU states on the corresponding small tries, every chronological cohort of 1–2 requests including duplicates/empty paths, and every history of 0–2 singleton cohorts. There are 468 and 2,520 decisions respectively. Of these, 576 have the entry state jointly reached from cold by precisely their supplied history. Other cross-product points test the decision function on reachable caches under counterfactual histories.
- Another 270 inputs have the entry reached by FCFS warm-up of the actual generated history, including shared prefixes and up to four requests per cohort. Fixed cases exercise all five legacy incumbent templates, a strict search win over the portfolio, differing resident sets, multiple actions sharing an exit, nonlexicographic incumbent retention, lexicographic ties after incumbent defeat, long paths at capacities 32 and 128, and every chronological history length through 16.
- A separate check over 134,640 reachable-state/historical-pair inputs compared direct stamped-leaf replay against the rank/identity equivalence and direct-score formula. It tested 61,227 subsequent encounters of an already-seen projection key and 65,648 cases meeting the sufficient direct-score test. Counts overlap. This finite check supplements the induction in the [derivation](derivation.md).
- In the 3,287 decision inputs, 2,361 had multiple immediate optima, 1,955 had multiple co-optimal actions sharing at least one complete exit, 344 had same-residency/different-recency alternatives, and 181 had differing resident sets. Incumbent retention overrode lexicographic order 9 times and lexicographic order settled 24 tied choices after the incumbent lost. The chosen objective strictly improved on the incumbent in 69 decisions.

The frozen binary and leaf oracle agreed on all 3,287 decisions. These counts concern artificial decisions. They do not estimate frequencies on a recorded trace or assign episode savings to recency or residency.

## Benchmark design review

The specified comparison uses one native binary for all four policies. All modes share the actual frozen State, transition and k-LPM selection logic. The native exact-immediate scheduler shares the current prefix-transition table, LCP-based lower bound, original portfolio priority and strict branch-and-bound improvement with both historical modes. It may stop as soon as the current incumbent attains the absent-union lower bound. Historical modes may use this exact-incumbent certificate but still inspect competing immediate-optimal full exits. k-LPM and immediate select without maintaining historical indexes. REF performs full-state pair rollouts, OPT may share exactly equal pair scores. The experiment design calls for fresh processes, bin resets, charged ingestion/history maintenance, caller wall/CPU, separately metered shared trace preparation, frozen repetition order and verification that each timed mode chooses its own archived trajectory. [Protocol](protocol.md)

The first compiled candidate passed the same 3,287 decisions against the independent oracle in all six native method/scoring modes. The production reset, warm-up and incremental group protocol also passed 546 measured decisions under two synthetic episodes per mode. The episodes deliberately reach the 16- and 32-cohort history-ring boundaries. The independent driver updates its chronological deque only after checking a group and compares both absolute and decision-only historical dispatches. One episode starts cold, another starts measured service after 19 FCFS warm-up groups. The first native pass used 0.944 s of wrapper CPU and 0.327 s of child CPU. Earlier frozen-only checks and the search for small fixtures used 0.866 CPU-s.

I read the timing driver and final native selection path without running the recorded workload or inspecting any comparative timing. Each treatment receives a fresh process per timing cell, a fresh session per bin, and only the current cohort after reset/warm-up. Its input paths are encoded before per-decision clocks. The history is method-local and causal. Both historical modes use the same enumeration and incumbent. The cheaper controls can take their own shortcuts. Indices, projection and memo construction belong to the optimized mode. The driver measures native parsing, selection and update; caller wall includes IPC and returned JSON. Process totals include wrapper logging, checks and warm-up. The history-bytes counter already includes the node-to-cohort index and is a storage estimate. The index can grow with the number of IDs seen in the bin. Memo bytes estimate per-pair vector storage, including allocated key capacity. Neither inventory measures full allocator or program residency, which is reported separately by peak RSS. The candidate-states counter is the number of distinct eligible exits actually carried into selection; a shortcut may report one. Visited current-order terminals and transition counts are recorded separately. [Native code](evaluator.cpp) · [Timing driver](benchmark.py)

Before freezing, inspection identified a long-session limit inherited from the frozen transition's unsigned stamp counter. Its previous lifetime was one decision; the new native session retains it between decisions. The final source clears stamps near overflow before the next command. At most 24 eligible exits, eight historical pairs and eight requests per pair bound the stamps used in a decision well below the 8,192 guard margin. The recorded bins could not approach this limit. The final binary also reports current prefix-transition cache allocation. Final pass CPU was 0.935 s wrapper plus 0.322 s child (1.380 s wall). Cumulative synthetic checks and fixture selection used 3.394 CPU-s.
